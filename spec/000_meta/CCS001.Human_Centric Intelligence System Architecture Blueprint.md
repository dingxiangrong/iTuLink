## Preface: Human-Centric as the Core, System-Enabled Empowerment
### I. The Call of the Era: Full-scale Breakthroughs and Development Foundations of AI Technology
Artificial intelligence has entered an era of comprehensive explosive growth. Breakthroughs across multiple fields have laid solid material and technical foundations for the full-scale application and systematic construction of intelligent technologies, becoming a core driving force for the advancement of intelligent society.

Large models have made rapid progress in language, image and multi-modal capabilities. Agents are maturing in autonomous thinking, task planning and tool invocation. Embodied intelligence has achieved leaps from perception-cognition to real-world execution. Robots are gradually entering real-life scenarios. Underlying hardware such as intelligent chips and general-domain computing power keep advancing. Multi-field technical breakthroughs jointly consolidate the technical foundation for building intelligent systems.

### II. Pain Points of Development: Deep-seated Contradictions and Path-related Risks in the Intelligence Era
Amid widespread AI technical breakthroughs, the development paradigm of the whole intelligence industry reveals deep-rooted problems extending beyond isolated sectoral conflicts into systemic risks concerning development direction, structural design and security. The industry generally falls into a technology-oriented misconception: prioritizing technology over human-centered values. Multiple core contradictions prevent intelligent technologies from truly serving society and the public.

First, misaligned value orientation: Intelligence is reduced to cold technical tools. Efforts focus on point-wise upgrades of models, devices and algorithms, while the ultimate mission of intelligence ¡ª *serving humans and empowering society* ¡ª is neglected, breaking away from original intentions.

Second, lack of systematic construction: Development remains fragmented and piecemeal. Models, devices and systems operate in isolation. There is no holistic architecture with cross-domain coordination and unified standards, failing to meet requirements for an inclusive, society-wide intelligent society.

Third, insufficient safety and controllability: Decision-making subjects are ambiguous. There is no human-centered top-level anchor, bringing risks such as intelligence alienation, technological runaway and privacy leakage, planting hidden dangers for future intelligent civilization.

These pain points indicate that current technical routes cannot support a safe, fair and human-warm intelligent era, and constitute the fundamental reason why we must re-evaluate civilizational directions.

Addressing industry-level pain points and corresponding social-development challenges and building on today¡¯s technical progress, this architecture abandons technology-centered thinking and adopts **human-centeredness** as its sole core logic. It constructs a state-society co-built, fully-covered, multi-layer linked Human-Centric Intelligence System. Intelligence returns to its origin of *serving humanity and empowering society*, resolving dual contradictions between industry demands and societal needs, and between technical advancement and social adaptation. The system¡¯s ultimate goals are **system co-construction, shared benefits for all, and full-domain empowerment**. Human subjectivity, decision sovereignty and personalized requirements run through design, operation and implementation. All layers operate collaboratively around human needs and social development.

Through this system, state-society-built general-domain intelligence capabilities precisely empower every individual and every social dimension. Human subjectivity is guaranteed at the foundational level while risks of intelligence alienation are fundamentally mitigated. It aims for a new form of intelligent civilization: human-centered, safe-and-controllable, and fully-collaborative, enabling intelligent technologies to become a genuine driver of social progress and human development.
