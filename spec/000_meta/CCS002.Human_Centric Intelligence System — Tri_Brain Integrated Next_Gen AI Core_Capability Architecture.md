# Human-Centric Intelligence System ¡ª Tri-Brain Integrated Next-Gen AI Core-Capability Architecture
**Cognition-Capability Connection Specification | CCS: Cognition Capability Specification**

## Abstract
Based on the top-level design of the Human-Centric Intelligence System and focusing on the implementation of core AI capabilities, this paper proposes a Tri-Brain-Integrated next-generation core-capability architecture for artificial intelligence. Guided by core design concepts: capability layering, individual-oriented subject, and standardized collaboration, it builds an AI core-capability system centered on **individuals (persons / enterprises), cross-domain collaborative, safe-and-controllable**.

The architecture forms a decoupled capability kernel consisting of Public-Brain, Specialized-Brain and Private-Brain. As the exclusive digital-will hub for individuals (persons / enterprises), the Private-Brain features four core functions: intent recognition, memory, decision-making and scheduling. It follows an evolution path from local deployment toward cloud deployment to finally realize genuine exclusiveness, sovereignty and persistence of individual-level intelligence.

As the core capability support of the Human-Centric Intelligence System for the AI domain, this architecture fundamentally solves key pain-points including tightly-coupled AI capabilities, absence of individual digital will, and uncontrollable privacy-sovereignty. It features high forward-looking vision, systemic completeness and industrial implementable value. It applies to future key scenarios: personal intelligence, enterprise-grade intelligence, industry-specific intelligence, cloud-native intelligence and human-machine cross-domain interaction, and provides core AI-domain capability support for the implementation of the top-level system.

**Keywords**: Human-Centric Intelligence System; Tri-Brain Integration; Public-Brain; Specialized-Brain; Private-Brain; Individual Intelligence; Enterprise-grade Intelligence; Digital Will; AI Core Capability; Individual Sovereignty

## I. Introduction
Artificial intelligence has entered a new phase characterized by cross-domain coverage, individualization and cloud-native convergence. Traditional centralized, platform-locked intelligent architectures expose fundamental defects: tight coupling between public and individual capabilities, lack of independent carriers for digital will of persons / enterprises, fragmented intelligent experience across devices and scenarios, and difficulties in guaranteeing data sovereignty and privacy security. Especially for enterprises as key market entities, demands for intelligent implementation of commercial-data sovereignty and business-decision will cannot be effectively satisfied.

Facing development trends of next-generation agents, non-perceptual interaction, cross-subject / cross-device collaboration and long-term autonomous evolution, and complying with top-level requirements of the Human-Centric Intelligence System (four-layer foundation with Tri-Brain as the core), the industry demands a logically-consistent, clearly-boundary, individual-oriented core-capability architecture for AI.

The Tri-Brain-Integrated architecture proposed in this paper offers a systematic solution to above-mentioned core challenges. As the AI-domain core-capability module derived from the Human-Centric Intelligence System, it collaborates with the independent Intelligence-Space layer in the top-level system. It delivers referable, evolvable and extensible core-capability support for future AI development, achieves deep inheritance and cross-domain coordination with the top-level system, and safeguards equal individual sovereignty and intelligent-implementation rights for both persons and enterprises.

## III. Tri-Brain Integration: Layered-design of AI-domain Core-Capability Kernel
The Tri-Brain-Integrated capability-kernel serves as core-capability support of Human-Centric Intelligence System for AI domain. It closely collaborates with the top-level Intelligence-Space layer. Layered-design realizes capability decoupling and precise empowerment. Acting as foundational capability base for AI, each layer takes its own responsibility and provides mutual support, with the Private-Brain as the absolute governor for all individuals (persons / enterprises).

### 3.1 Public-Brain: Public-intelligence Foundation
The Public-Brain is a universal-benefit public intelligent infrastructure for the whole society. It represents the implementation of public capabilities of Human-Centric Intelligence System within AI domain. As the foundational-capability layer of Tri-Brain Integration, it provides standardized, universal-benefit foundational capability support for the whole AI system and equally serves all persons and enterprises.

**Core Capability Scope**: Provides general knowledge repository, public-service integration, basic logical reasoning, general tool-invocation and other undifferentiated public-intelligence capabilities. It complies with cross-domain unified capability-output standards, adapts to connection & transmission requirements of Intelligence-Space, and delivers foundational-capability support for Specialized-Brain and Private-Brain (for persons / enterprises).

**Core Positioning**: A ¡°public-capability pool¡± in AI domain. It carries no individual-exclusive attributes, opens to all persons and enterprises, and realizes shared and high-efficiency utilization of public-intelligence resources across individuals and enterprises.

### 3.2 Specialized-Brain: Industry-specific Professional Intelligence
The Specialized-Brain targets vertical domains and industry scenarios. It represents industry-empowerment implementation of Human-Centric Intelligence System within AI domain. As the professional-capability layer of Tri-Brain Integration, it facilitates deep integration between AI and real economy, and delivers precise professional-capability support for individual industry-oriented demands and enterprise core-business requirements.

**Core Capability Scope**: Delivers in-depth industry knowledge, scenario-oriented business logic, customized solutions, vertical-business execution and other professional-intelligence capabilities. It is finely layered by industry attributes (e.g. Industrial-Specialized-Brain, Medical-Specialized-Brain, Financial-Specialized-Brain, Retail-Specialized-Brain). It follows industry-unified capability-output standards, can be scheduled on-demand by Private-Brain of persons / enterprises, and meanwhile invokes foundational public-capability support from Public-Brain.

**Core Positioning**: An ¡°industry-capability engine¡± in AI domain. It focuses on industry pain-points and industry-oriented demands of persons and enterprises, realizes precise empowerment of professional capabilities, and builds an industry-bridge connecting public intelligence and individual-level intelligence of persons / enterprises.

### 3.3 Private-Brain: Exclusive Digital-Will Hub for Individuals (Persons / Enterprises)
The Private-Brain is an exclusive digital-will hub fully belonging to independent persons or enterprises. It is the only core subject representing ¡°individuals¡± inside the intelligence system. It is not bound to devices, not owned by platforms, and persists regardless of terminal or organizational-structure changes. It embodies the core ¡°individual-centered¡± philosophy of Human-Centric Intelligence System in AI domain, and acts as the absolute governor within Tri-Brain-Integration system.

- **Personal Private-Brain**: Focuses on personalized demands in personal life, work and study, and carries personal digital-persona and will.
- **Enterprise Private-Brain**: Focuses on commercial demands including enterprise operation, decision-making, production and management. It undertakes enterprise-commercial sovereignty and decision-making will, adapts to enterprise organizational-structure and business scale, and guarantees exclusive controllability over enterprise commercial data and core decisions.

As the core of Tri-Brain Integration, both Personal Private-Brain and Enterprise Private-Brain own four core functions:

1. **Intent Recognition**: Precisely comprehend and parse real demands & objectives of **individuals (persons / enterprises)**, capture core will (personal personalized will / enterprise commercial-decision will), and generate unambiguous demand instructions.
2. **Memory**: Accumulate behavioral habits, personalized preferences / business rules, business data and development outcomes of **individuals (persons / enterprises)**. It forms digital-memory and digital-subject features exclusive to each individual (personal digital-persona / enterprise commercial traits), and provides core basis for decision-making.
3. **Decision-making**: Based on individual intent and exclusive memory, generate autonomous judgments and executable action-plans conforming to will of **individuals (persons / enterprises)**, and meanwhile complete compliance-validation against individual authorization boundaries (personal-privacy compliance / enterprise-business and data compliance).
4. **Scheduling**: According to decision outcomes, organize and coordinate invocation of Public-Brain public capabilities and Specialized-Brain industry-specific capabilities. It realizes exclusive integration and execution of capabilities matching individual demands. Enterprise Private-Brain supports large-scale capability scheduling and multi-scenario collaborative-execution requirements.

**Core Responsibilities of Private-Brain**: Safeguard individual-data sovereignty, privacy / commercial security and implementation of core will for persons and enterprises. It serves as the ¡°core brain¡± of the whole AI core-capability architecture. All capability invocations and collaborations are initiated and governed by Private-Brain, which controls operation direction and outcomes of individual-level intelligence throughout the process. Enterprise Private-Brain additionally undertakes responsibilities for enterprise commercial-sovereignty guarantee, business-process collaboration and large-scale intelligent implementation.

## IV. Private-Brain Evolution Path: From Local toward Cloud
As the core of Tri-Brain Integration, both Personal Private-Brain and Enterprise Private-Brain follow a phased evolution path migrating from local deployment toward individual-exclusive private-cloud environments, and finally become stable, independent and continuously-evolving carriers for individual digital-will. Enterprise Private-Brain supports distributed deployment and large-scale evolution according to business needs. The whole evolution strictly complies with top-level requirements ¡°safe-and-controllable, individual-governed¡± of Human-Centric Intelligence System, leverages cross-domain-connection capabilities of Intelligence-Space, and guarantees continuity and consistency of user-experience during evolution.

### Three Evolution Phases
1. **Local Initial Phase**: Light-weight Private-Brain (Personal instance runs on personal terminals such as mobile phones and PCs; Enterprise instance runs on enterprise on-premise servers and intranet terminals). It implements basic intent-recognition, individual-memory, simple decision-making and local-capability scheduling. All individual data (personal-privacy data / enterprise-commercial data) is stored locally to guarantee data-security and sovereignty-controllability. It can connect to local Intelligence-Space nodes for basic interaction.

2. **Local-Cloud Hybrid Phase**: Core capabilities of Private-Brain gradually migrate toward individual-exclusive private-cloud environments (personal exclusive cloud space / enterprise private cloud). Individual-memory and decision-logic realize local-cloud synchronization. Enterprise Private-Brain enables multi-node and cross-department capability-collaboration and data-synchronization. Through Intelligence-Space, cross-device, cross-scenario and cross-department capability-scheduling and collaboration are realized, balancing data-security and cross-domain user-experience.

3. **Cloud-native Mature Phase**: Private-Brain runs independently within individual-exclusive private-cloud environments with complete capabilities of intent-recognition, memory, decision-making and scheduling. (Enterprise Private-Brain adapts to large-scale, professional and distributed decision-making & scheduling capabilities.) Through Intelligence-Space, it achieves high-efficiency cross-domain collaboration with Public-Brain and Specialized-Brain. Individual-level intelligence for persons / enterprises becomes fully exclusive, sovereign, persistent and cross-domain. Individual-data remains autonomously controlled by Private-Brain throughout; Enterprise Private-Brain guarantees exclusive ownership and usage rights of commercial data.

### Core Evolution Objectives
Aligned with top-level design, realize exclusiveness, cross-domain capability and persistence for individual-level intelligence of persons / enterprises, and become stable digital-will carriers for all individuals inside digital-world:

- **True Exclusiveness**: Private-Brain belongs to independent persons / enterprises instead of any device or platform. It fully implements the ¡°individual-centered¡± core philosophy and guarantees independent sovereignty of enterprise-commercial intelligence.
- **Persistence**: Individual memory, will and decision-logic (personal digital-persona / enterprise-business rules & decision-making system) are accumulated long-term and continuously evolved, forming irreplaceable digital-subject traits for each individual.
- **Cross-domain Consistency**: Relying on cross-domain-connection capabilities of top-level Intelligence-Space, a unified set of individual-level intelligence delivers consistent experience across all scenarios, devices and departments without fragmentation or discontinuity. Enterprise Private-Brain supports cross-organization and cross-scenario cross-domain commercial-intelligence collaboration.

## V. Architecture Operation Logic
As a core sub-architecture of Human-Centric Intelligence System, its operation-logic fully inherits top-level design and closely collaborates with top-level Intelligence-Space layer. The full workflow follows principle: **Private-Brain-governed, layered-empowered, Space-connected, individual-sovereignty-oriented**. It satisfies intelligent-demands of both persons and enterprises and realizes high-efficiency scheduling & execution of AI-domain core-capabilities:

1. **Individuals (persons / enterprises)** initiate interaction and demand-input through top-level Intelligence-Space layer (personal personalized demands / enterprise-commercial demands). Intelligence-Space purely forwards information to individual-exclusive Private-Brain without any logical processing.
2. Private-Brain performs intent-recognition to accurately parse real demands of **individuals (persons / enterprises)**, and invokes individual-exclusive memory-data (personal-privacy data / enterprise-commercial data) as basis for demand-analysis.
3. Based on intent and exclusive memory, Private-Brain makes autonomous decisions and generates executable action-plans (light-weight plans for individuals / large-scale professional plans for enterprises). It clarifies scope & paths for invoking Public-Brain / Specialized-Brain capabilities and completes compliance-validation.
4. Through Intelligence-Space, Private-Brain initiates unified scheduling for Public-Brain public-capabilities and Specialized-Brain industry-specific capabilities according to decision outcomes and sends execution instructions. Enterprise Private-Brain supports simultaneous scheduling and collaborative-execution for multi-capability and multi-node tasks.
5. After receiving instructions, Public-Brain and Specialized-Brain execute corresponding capabilities and return execution outcomes back to individual-exclusive Private-Brain via Intelligence-Space.
6. Private-Brain integrates and processes execution outcomes to generate final results matching demands of **individuals (persons / enterprises)** (personal-customized results / enterprise-commercial deliverables), and presents final outcomes to individuals through Intelligence-Space.
7. Private-Brain updates memory for this interaction (including personal / enterprise demands, decision-making process, execution outcomes, business-data etc.), realizing continuous evolution of digital-will and capabilities for **individuals (persons / enterprises)**.

### Core Operation Rules
Strictly comply with top-level-design requirements, clarify capability-boundaries for three-brain system and Intelligence-Space. Guarantee safe, controllable and high-efficiency system-operation while meeting sovereignty and implementation-demands for both persons and enterprises:

1. **Private-Brain governs full workflow**: As absolute core of Tri-Brain Integration, Private-Brain (personal or enterprise) governs the whole workflow including intent-recognition, memory invocation, decision-making and scheduling. All capability-invocations originate from Private-Brain; Public-Brain and Specialized-Brain own no active decision-making authority.
2. **Decoupled execution for three-brain capabilities**: Public-Brain only provides public foundational-capabilities; Specialized-Brain only provides industry-specific professional-capabilities; Private-Brain is only responsible for will-implementation and capability-scheduling of **individuals (persons / enterprises)**. Capabilities are decoupled without mutual intervention. Enterprise Private-Brain can implement large-scale integration based on decoupled capabilities.
3. **Standardized capability-invocation**: Both Public-Brain and Specialized-Brain comply with cross-domain unified capability-output standards and adapt to connection-requirements of Intelligence-Space, guaranteeing high-efficiency and compatible scheduling by Private-Brain of persons / enterprises. Enterprise Private-Brain supports large-scale invocation of standardized capabilities.
4. **Controllable privacy & data-sovereignty**: Individual-data and memory of persons / enterprises remain autonomously controlled by Private-Brain throughout workflow. Public-Brain and Specialized-Brain never access or store personal-privacy data or enterprise core-commercial data. Intelligence-Space only forwards information without data-storage permission, safeguarding enterprise commercial-data sovereignty.
5. **Cross-domain collaboration relies on Intelligence-Space**: All interactions and data-transmission among Tri-Brain components, and between Tri-Brain and persons / enterprises are completed through top-level Intelligence-Space layer. It realizes standardized collaboration under mode of ¡°Capability-Kernel + Connection-Hub¡±. Enterprise Private-Brain supports cross-department and cross-scenario cross-domain commercial-intelligence collaboration.
6. **Individual-adaptation principle**: The architecture adapts to different intelligent-demand characteristics for persons and enterprises. Personal Private-Brain runs in lightweight and personalized mode; Enterprise Private-Brain runs in large-scale and professional mode. Both share capability base of Public-Brain and Specialized-Brain and equally enjoy individual-sovereignty.
