**Cognition-Capability Connection Specification | CCS: Cognition Capability Specification**

> Position Statement:
> This architecture is not a single piece of software, not a standalone application, nor an ordinary application-development pattern.

Various businesses, operations-maintenance, devices, knowledge and communication capabilities are derivative artifacts built upon this system. The system defines how humans extend perception and action outward via technology, and establishes covenants for sovereignty, authorization, transfer and execution between humans and machines.

> Core Missions of this architecture:
> - **SV (Sovereign Vessel) handles connection and sovereignty-boundary governance within Intelligence-Space**
> - **CDT (Cognition-Deduction-Tree) together with SAM (Sovereign Aggregate Memory) realize the engineering implementation of the Private-Brain**

## I. Core General Principles of the Architecture
This architecture re-thinks traditional Agent and Skill tool-invocation paradigms.

Traditional paradigm: Treat AI as an independent enhanced application;
This paradigm: Treat humans as sovereign subjects. The whole technical system serves humans and acts as an extension carrier for human capability expansion.

### Four Foundational Axioms (Permanently Locked)
1. **Sovereignty belongs to humans; AI executes under authorization**: Humans hold final decision-making authority over the system. AI possesses no native autonomy and only receives limited autonomy granted by human sovereignty to conduct deduction and execution within authorization boundaries. Technology is an extension of human capabilities and shall not replace human subject status.

2. **Complete decoupling of thinking and execution**: The Private-Brain only thinks; CC only executes. The cognition layer shall not access resources; the execution layer shall not generate reasoning. The separation of thinking and execution ensures humans can intervene, interrupt or halt processes at any stage.

3. **Reusable technology with strong semantic isolation**: Underlying technical components may be shared; upper-layer architectural roles, hierarchies, sovereignty and responsibilities are strictly differentiated. Entities shall not be merged merely because underlying technologies share the same origin.

4. **All capabilities containerized**: Global businesses, systems, hosts, devices, knowledge and communication capabilities are uniformly encapsulated into CC containers with standardized execution closed-loops. Every category of capability extended outward by humans can be governed, verified and revoked.

### Global Standard Five-Layer Architecture (Top-down non-reversible)
**SE Sovereign-Entry Layer ¡ú Port Global-Access Layer ¡ú SV Sovereign-Vessel Layer ¡ú Private-Brain (CDT & SAM) Cognition-Deduction Layer ¡ú Intelligence-Space CC-Cluster Execution Layer**
Supporting Foundation: SAM Sovereign Aggregate Memory

> Interpretation of non-reversible hierarchy constraint: Upper-layer entities may initiate invocations and issue events downwards. Lower-layer entities are prohibited from actively invoking upwards. Result callbacks and event reporting count as reply back-flows rather than active upward invocations from lower layers. Reply back-flows must strictly follow the original return path. SV is only allowed to perform pass-through caching of deduced results. SV must not actively trigger the Private-Brain to start deduction to avoid cross-layer over-privileged operations.

> Allow-list for upward channels (exceptions to the non-reversibility rule)
> This allow-list is exhaustive. Any upward behavior not listed below is prohibited.
> 1. Task reply back-flow: After a lower layer receives an upper-layer request, it returns the corresponding reply message along the original path (including normal results, business exceptions and business errors).
> 2. Event-reporting back-flow: Derived events triggered by instructions issued from upper layers are passed upward along the original link.
> 3. SV pass-through caching: SV only performs message pass-through caching. It must not actively invoke the Private-Brain or generate new deductions.
> 4. Error-alert reporting: Business failures or runtime exceptions occurring during CC execution can only flow upward as attached payloads within the corresponding task context. CC shall not independently initiate brand-new upward requests.
> 5. Liveness heartbeat reporting: Component liveness status and keep-alive heartbeats may only be reported to SV, and must not penetrate directly to the Private-Brain or SE Sovereign-Entry Layer. SV aggregates and forwards them upward in a unified manner.
> 6. Passive authorization-change callback: Signals of authorization modification or authorization expiry are pushed downwards from SV to CC. CC only updates local status and must not actively poll / query SE authorization status upwards.

## II. SE Sovereign-Entry Layer (Highest Global Layer)
### Core Definition
The sole highest-sovereignty domain of the Human-Centric Intelligence System, representing the human subject.
The whole system is an extension for humans to expand capabilities outwards; all volition originates from humans.
- Humans are the final arbiters for all decisions
- All autonomy and execution permissions of AI are derived from authorizations issued by SE
- The start-point, end-point, veto-power and termination-power for interactions belong to SE

### Core Rights & Responsibilities
Authorization, revocation of authority, intervention, rejection, termination, observation, perception.
By granting authorizations, humans project their own volition into the technical system and extend their perception and action boundaries leveraging technology.

## III. Port Global-Access Layer (Unique Human-Machine Gateway)
### Core Iron Rule
Port belongs to the SE sovereignty layer; it does not belong to any CC or the execution layer.
The sole responsibility of Port: bidirectional normalization conversion between heterogeneous signals from the external world and internal unified meta-language messages.
All real-world access paths are normalized via Port, serving as contact points for humans entering and exiting Intelligence-Space.

### Global Standard Ports
1. Web-Port: Browser-based user access
2. PC-Port: Local-terminal host user access
3. Com-Port: Global-communication-media access (Enterprise-WeChat / SMS / Email / IM inbound)

### Key Architectural Isolation
- Com-Port: Human inbound (sovereign access channel)
- Com-CC: System outbound execution (execution capability for human outward capability-extension)
Underlying technologies may share the same origin, while architectural semantics are thoroughly isolated.

## IV. SV Sovereign Vessel (Unique Global Hub)

## VI. Intelligence-Space CC-Container Cluster (Global Execution Layer, Deployable Entities)
### Unified Standard Paradigm for CC (all containers shall comply)
1. Standardized autonomous capability containers
2. Task life-cycle management
3. Definition of Action-Items (AC)
4. prepare ¡ú check ¡ú submit three-phase execution closed-loop
5. All execution permissions originate from SE sovereign authorization

### Two Major CC Categories
1. Business-oriented CC: For upper-layer business logic
2. System-Device-oriented CC: For host systems, hardware and operation-maintenance governance

#### (I) Business-oriented CC
##### 1. Spring-CC | Server-side business-application container
- Runtime domain: Back-end service-application layer
- Managed objects: Enterprise businesses, business Services, database business data, form workflows, business approvals
- Constraints: Process business semantics only; must not access server system commands or hardware resources

##### 2. Web-CC | Browser business-application container
- Runtime domain: Browser Web sandbox
- Managed objects: Front-end page components, Web business interactions, front-end application capabilities
- Constraints: Host upper-layer Web businesses only; must not manipulate underlying system privileges

##### 3. KB-CC | Knowledge-base business container
- Runtime domain: Global-scope general
- Managed objects: Knowledge classification, document management, permission-controlled retrieval, knowledge closed-loop workflows
- Isolation rule: Underlying storage technologies may be reused with SAM, while semantics are strictly isolated
- SAM: Human-owned sovereign memory; KB-CC: Public objective knowledge

##### 4. Com-CC | Global-communication business container
- Runtime domain: Global-scope general
- Managed objects: System outbound push, message delivery, Email / SMS / Enterprise-WeChat outbound delivery
- Isolation rule: Inbound messages go through Com-Port; outbound execution goes through Com-CC

#### (II) System-Device-oriented CC
> Classification logic:
> Host series: General-purpose computer hosts with homogeneous command-sets;
> Device-CC: Non-general-purpose hosts, heterogeneous peripherals and embedded hardware.

##### 1. Host-CC | General-purpose host-system container
- Coverage objects: Linux servers, Windows servers and various business hosts
- Managed capabilities: System commands, processes, disk operation-maintenance, resource monitoring, host files
- Distinction: Spring-CC runs businesses on top of hosts; Host-CC governs the host system itself.

##### 2. PC-CC | Local-user-terminal host container
- Coverage objects: Laptops, desktops and local user terminals
- Managed capabilities: Local files, local host commands, terminal scripts, personal host resources
- Belongs to the Host homologous system, targeting end-user terminal form-factors

##### 3. Device-CC | Heterogeneous peripheral-hardware container
- Coverage objects: Embedded devices, IoT sensors, externally-connected non-standard hardware
- Characteristics: Device heterogeneity with diverse protocols; not general-purpose computer hosts

## VIII. Global-Standard Operation Closed-Loop (Deployable Execution Workflow)
1. Human via SE sets authorization boundaries
2. External signals are normalized into internal meta-language messages via Port and delivered to SV
3. SV forwards messages carrying authorization context to Private-Brain (CDT) for cognition-deduction
4. Private-Brain outputs standardized instruct instructions. SV routes and dispatches them to corresponding CC according to path
- Business requests ¡ú Business-oriented CC
- Host-operation-maintenance operations ¡ú Host-CC / PC-CC
- Hardware-device manipulation ¡ú Device-CC
5. CC executes the full prepare-check-submit closed-loop and validates permissions and execution results
6. Objective phenomena generated by execution flow back along the original path and are fed back to human sovereign entry SE via Port

> Including message-envelope payloads, authorization context, path / event_type and instruction fields.
